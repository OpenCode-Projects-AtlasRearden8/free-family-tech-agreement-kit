My First Phone — a Contract to Sign Together

A phone is a big deal, so we sign for it like one. Read every line out loud, kid and grown-up taking turns. Either of you may cross out a line and talk about a different deal. A crossed-out line with both initials next to it is respected — that's how contracts work.

What I, the kid, agree to

  • I will not add apps, delete apps, or change any setting without asking a grown-up first.
  • I will answer when a grown-up calls or texts me, even just to say "going to practice, be home at 7."
  • My phone charges overnight in the family charging spot — never in my bedroom.
  • I will not use the phone to be mean, embarrass anyone, or join in on a joke at someone else's expense, even if everyone else is doing it.
  • If something upsetting comes across my screen, I keep it (I won't delete it in a panic) and I show a grown-up. Telling them is always the safest move.
  • I know that "everyone at school has one" is a reason to talk about it, not a rule that overrides this contract.
  • Any new social media account requires a grown-up conversation before signing up, not after.

What I, the grown-up, agree to

  • I will not read through messages for fun or curiosity. I may check the phone when I have a real, specific worry, and I will tell you when I do.
  • If you call me scared or in trouble — whether it's midnight or a party gone weird — I will pick you up first and talk about it later. The ride home is never a punishment session.
  • I will not take your phone away as a first response to every mistake; we follow the consequences we wrote down together.
  • I will tell you honestly if I ever have reason to check, and I will not pretend I didn't.
