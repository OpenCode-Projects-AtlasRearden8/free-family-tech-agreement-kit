Evening Device-Curfew Menus

Too many rules cause fights; a menu causes choices. Print one page, circle tonight's plan, and post it on the fridge. Each menu runs from after-school to lights-out for one evening. Choose the menu before 4 p.m., not mid-argument.

Menu A — Quiet Wind-Down (circle one)

  • School & chores first, then 45 minutes of screens, finished by  
  • Devices go to the charging spot at  
  • After screens: reading show, drawing, puzzle, or talking — pick one

Menu B — Homework Marathon

  • Screens hold off completely until homework is fully done and shown to a grown-up
  • Then 30 minutes of screens where you choose the thing
  • Devices go to the charging spot at  

Menu C — Screen-Free Special

  • No fun screens tonight — on purpose, not as punishment
  • Choose one instead: board game, walk, baking, build something, backyard
  • Devices still charge in the same spot at the same time

Menu D — Earned Extra

  • If chores, homework, and kindness all happened today: +15 minutes beyond Menu A
  • If any of those didn't happen: Menu A applies, no negotiating tonight

Menu E — Chill for Grown-Ups (low-energy day)

  • Everyone is tired, so screens are quiet-only: headphones on, one shared show, or a calm game
  • Same charging-spot time as always — routine beats rules
