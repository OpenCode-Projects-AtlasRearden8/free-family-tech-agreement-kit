Grandparent-Visit Screen Rules

Visits to special grown-ups are the exact wrong place for daily screen battles, and the exact right place for a simple pre-made plan. Fill this out once, share it with the grandparents before the visit, and everyone gets to enjoy the visit for real.

Ground rules we all accept

  • Kid brings their own tablet/device from home when the grandparents agree — or leaves it home entirely (check one):
    ☐ bring    ☐ leave home    ☐ only for the car ride
  • Any screens used at the grandparents' house run on their WiFi, their rules — grandparents may veto any app, show, or volume from their own couch.
  • If the iPad is turned on, it is together-time: kid sits with the grown-up and shows or shares something, it is not a babysitter.
  • Being bored at Grandma's is allowed and is not an emergency. Boredom is where good stories and cookie-making come from.
  • Nobody guilt-trips anybody. If a rule needs to bend, the grandparents and parents discuss it in the kitchen first.

The special-visit treats (grandparents get to own this list)

  • Extra screen time for a shared thing the grandparents pick: a movie, a show from their decade, a video call with cousins.
  • One photo/video may be taken by the kid for the family — saved and shared with parents after the visit.

Video call rule: Calling the grandparents from the family home counts toward the everyday screen menu (Menu on the Evenings page). Calling grandma is the screen use, not an add-on.
