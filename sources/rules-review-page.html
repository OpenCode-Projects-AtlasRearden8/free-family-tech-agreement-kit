Review Date Page — Rules That Grow With You

Agreements that never change are agreements that get fought over. Put a standing review date on the calendar before the rules are even finished, and both kid and grown-up know that "amendments" are normal, planned, and earned — not a crisis in the moment.

How it works

  • Pick a day: every first Saturday, a school holiday, or the kid's "half birthday." Whatever is easy to remember wins.
  • Amendments both ways: kids may propose a change at a review (more time, new app); grown-ups may propose one too (earlier curfew, new chore).
  • Everything else is frozen between reviews. "Can we change the curfew just for tonight?" is answered with "no, but ask me at the review — that's what it's for."
  • Serving suggestion: pick a snack for the meeting. Rules taste better with cookies.
